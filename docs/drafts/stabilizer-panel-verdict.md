## Как это называть

Не «stabiliser», не «damper», не «market maker», не «support» — ни на странице, ни в реестре, ни в дневнике: ни один кошелёк не действует по цене, ни один уровень не защищается, и слово обещало бы поведение цены, которого дизайн не даёт. Честное имя: «the arm's envelope» — опубликованная граница того, что арм МОЖЕТ сделать с ценой сам (≤2.0% за сделку, ≤+9.8%/−9.4% между любыми двумя моментами при сегодняшних резервах, пересчитывается живьём), а не того, что цена БУДЕТ делать. Kicker страницы остаётся «Disclosed experiment · wash trading»; в lede добавляется «with a published bound on its own footprint». Если Tier-A демпфер когда-нибудь будет построен — называть его так, как предложил external-flow-only: «issuer takes the other side», не «стабилизатор».

## Черновик поправки (для rules.json, на языке файла)

FIFTEENTH AMENDMENT, 17 Sep 2026: the arm's own amplitude is turned down, and the stabiliser that was on the table is not built. (The tenth was applied and reverted on 29 Aug; its number stays burnt.) The proposal was a second founder wallet — BYKO LP Pumper, 0x9c86…3206, already in the register — trading against price moves of 15% at once, 10% over a day or 5% over a week, damping three quarters of the move, with a public log. It fails on its own arithmetic before it fails on optics. Under this file as it stands the byko arm alone prints a 12–27% daily high–low range (287 confirmed trades, 8–17 Sep), a single cap-sized trade at 2.5% of pool USDC moves the price 5.05%, a double 10.4%, and the cash corridor the wallet may wander — run_floor_usdc 1.5 to run_ceiling_usdc 40, plus the overshoots and the fallback in drawRunTarget that could push a target a further $5 out — permits $59 of one-way flow, which at 296 USDC of reserve is +44% or −36%. Every one of the stabiliser's thresholds sits inside what the first wallet is allowed to do, so the second wallet would have spent most of its days undoing the first, with the price fed back into a decision — which the ninth amendment removed from this file on purpose; it holds no USDC, so it could only ever sell; and the outside trades it would meet are the few a week that the eighth amendment calls the experiment working, into which it would have sold three quarters of every purchase. The swing is not weather; it is three numbers in this file, and they change: (1) run_floor_usdc goes [1.5, 8] to [6, 8] and run_ceiling_usdc goes [20, 40] to [11, 13]; (2) max_trade_pct_pool goes 2.5 to 1.0; (3) in the worker, drawRunTarget's fallback — when the drawn edge sat within $0.50 of the balance it pushed the target a further $1–5 out, so a sell run could aim up to $5 above the published ceiling — becomes a fixed $0.50, so the corridor in this file is the corridor the code draws. Nothing else moves: trade_usdc stays [0.30, 9] with the cap binding it, which is the third amendment's principle that the instrument scales to what it measures; the three cadence modes, the six-hour gap ceiling, the log-uniform sizes and waits, the contrarian 20, skip 15, double 8, early-reversal 5 and spike 5 coins, the $10,000 gross-spend backstop and the gas floor are untouched, and nothing reads the price. What the numbers buy, stated as arithmetic and not as a promise: a single trade at the cap moves the price 2.0% at any depth, a double 4.0%; the wallet's cash can wander only between $6 and $14.25 before the last trade's overshoot at either end, so the arm's net flow between any two moments is bounded by $14.17 at today's cap, which at 296 USDC of reserve is +9.8% or −9.4%. That figure is recomputed from the live reserves and printed on the page beside the rules, because it scales with the pool (about +12.6% at $200 of reserve, +15.5% at $150, +7.4% at $500) and holds only while nothing is deposited into an arm's wallet, which the chain would show. A simulation of this exact cycle against 296 USDC : 185,254 BYKO with no outside trades gives about 33 trades a day as now, gross flow near $42 a day instead of about $80, per-trade impact median 0.65% and never above 2.0%, and a daily high–low range with a median near 5%, under 8% on nine days in ten and a worst day near 12%; the same simulation under-predicts the current rule's measured range by about a fifth, so the honest expectation is a median of 5–6%, under about 8–10% on nine days in ten, and the page prints each day's realised range so these figures can be graded against the tape. The rollover, disclosed because it is not nothing: all four wallets hold more cash than the new ceiling (byko $23.15, luko $34.30, luko01 $17.29, luko02 $20.04 as this is written); rather than let the envelope force a buy run from each — about $16 into the BYKO pool and about $50 into LUKO on the day of the amendment, an 11% and a 25% step that would read as a pump — the excess is withdrawn to the ops wallets in the register before the hash rolls, the transfers are published, and direction and run_target_usdc are cleared so each first run is drawn fresh under this rule. The strategy block is shared, so the three LUKO arms take the same three numbers: per arm the bound on the deeper LUKO pool is about +7%, but three unsynchronised arms can add to about +21%, so the control is the same rule at three times the intensity, not the same amplitude, and its page says so. Published beside the rule, as an instrument and not a rule: an attribution of every swap in both pools — this worker's own by transaction hash, the founder's by the register, the rest outside — so the share of BYKO's volume that is the author is a chain-derived number rather than a claim; if trading against outside flow is ever proposed again, the case will be made from that ledger and from the bound above, in its own amendment, with thresholds that sit above the bound. Consequences stated in advance: the mean trade falls from about $2.40 to about $1.16, so the pool becomes more, not less, 'dominated by tiny trades' in the words of Blockaid's reply; the 24-hour volume the aggregators show roughly halves, and the thirteenth amendment's worry that a quiet chart reads as dead comes a step closer; an outside $20 buy still moves this pool 13.9% and nothing here will undo it — the step persists and the arm oscillates around wherever outside flow leaves the price; and the prediction of 19 Aug stands unchanged — the unstable-price flag will not clear, because the condition is depth and this amendment does not touch depth. Same honesty clause as the sixth, seventh, ninth and twelfth, in a new dimension: those shaped the tape's texture, this one shrinks its amplitude, and a calmer chart made of one address trading with itself is what a wash trader who had read the classifier's wording would produce — arguably more misleading to a naive viewer than a wild one — and this file says so before the first trade under the new rule. One more thing, said plainly: the worker still reads no price, but the price reached the author. These numbers change because of an observed price output, by hand, ten days after the twelfth and thirteenth set them; that is the same loop with a human in it and a period of weeks. ## Риски, которые остаются

- 100% движения цены по-прежнему поток автора; спокойный wash-график, возможно, вводит в заблуждение наивного читателя сильнее дикого — honesty clause это говорит, и это подрывает «стабильное» прочтение, которого хотел владелец.
- Blockaid: эффекта нет и не предсказано; если флаг всё же уйдёт, вывод неудобный — токен, не изменивший ничего, стал чистым, потому что эмитент сделал свой wash trading тише.
- Петля с человеком: цена дошла до автора; это четвёртая перекройка коридора, и читатель вправе назвать это parameter-fiddling.
- Объём падает вдвое — риск «мёртвого графика» из тринадцатой поправки становится ближе; аггрегаторы покажут $40/день.
- Граница масштабируется как 1/U: при $150 в пуле это уже +15.5%; внешний дамп ухудшает её в тот момент, когда владельцу хотелось бы обратного. Публикуется живьём, но обещать «под 10%» нельзя.
- Любая внешняя сделка $10–20 превышает весь дневной диапазон арма и сохраняется навсегда — арм никогда её не откатывает; на странице это надо сказать, иначе первый же реальный покупатель прочитается как «правило не работает».
- Симуляция занижает замер текущего правила на ~20%; опубликованные ожидания несут эту ошибку и должны печататься с ней.
- Проверяемость «девяти дней из десяти» зависит от архива (export.mjs ни разу не коммитился) и от новой per-day таблицы в API; до этого — только чейн.
- LUKO: три арма = совокупная граница +21%, размах не сопоставим с BYKO; контроль остаётся контролем для классификаторов, не для размаха.
- Rollover: все четыре арма стоят на rules-mismatch между deploy и D1-строкой; вывод USDC — founder-транзакции, которые читатель должен увидеть объяснёнными до того, как увидит их на BaseScan.
- Слив комиссии ~$0.13/день: первый халт token-dust/usdc-dust через 4–5 месяцев; долив кошелька нарушит «no arm needs topping up» и потребует записи.
- Нет демонстрации «бот защищает цену» — владелец теряет то, что хотел показать; получает только before/after, который менее shareable.
- Ledger классифицирует основателя с нераскрытого адреса как outside, 4337-кошельки и агрегаторы — по tx.from; полнота реестра остаётся допущением и должна быть названа.
