3. Is there any route for a token that is structurally, permanently small, or

To qualify for reassessment, a token must meet specific liquidity and volume
criteria. The primary requirements are to substantially deepen liquidity in
the main trading pool so the asset is no longer thinly capitalized, and to
sustain healthier two-sided trading activity to prevent price manipulation
from micro-trades.
