The contract is the specification.

The document is written and versioned in the repository — every change to it is a commit, so every revision is traceable. Where the document and the deployed contract disagree, the contract prevails: 21 lines, fixed supply of 790,227, no owner, immutable.

SectionWhat it fixes
1 · TokenName, symbol, standard, decimals, supply, network, contract address.
2 · InvariantsWhat cannot change for the lifetime of the chain: supply fixed, nobody in charge, code final, source public.
3 · Deployment recordCreation transaction, block, date, deployer, compiler, verification.
4 · Genesis distributionThe single mint, divided once: 93.7% to the pool, 6.3% to the founder.
5 · MarketPool, genesis ratio, and the LP burn — burned, not locked, with both transactions.
6 · Donation cardsDonation address, issuance, contents, permanence. A card confers no rights.
7 · Settlement in BYKOInvoices priced in euro, payable in BYKO at the pool rate of a named block. One person's decision; the token is unchanged by it.
8 · Public read APIsThe endpoints this site exposes. They read chain state and never write to it.
9 · VersioningEvery revision is a commit; the changelog is part of the document.

Version 1.3 added §7. What it does not do is change the token: the contract still has no built-in functionality and gives its holder no rights.

It does not represent equity, debt or any claim on anything. Nothing on this page is financial advice.
