Stabilizer

loading…

One founder wallet watches the BYKO pool every ten minutes. When a trade of under $100 by somebody outside the published register moves the price more than 5% away from the reference, the wallet trades back three quarters of the move. This project's own trades carry the reference with them; its own corrections, any founder trade and any outside trade of $100 or more reset it.

It sells into outside buying and buys into outside selling, so it earns the spread; every look and every decision is logged below. The self-trading arm keeps running beside it.
