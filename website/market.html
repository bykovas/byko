Denominations · computed

What a euro buys

Fourteen euro amounts on a running tape, and a calculator for any other — each figure the pool's own constant-product answer, the same maths getAmountsOut runs, with the 0.3% fee and the price impact already in it, worked out in your browser from the reserves. This is deliberately not the price above multiplied out: the pool answers one swap at a time, and the larger the order the worse the rate it gets, which is why fifteen hundred euro does not buy fifteen hundred times what one euro buys. Nothing here is a quote or an offer.
