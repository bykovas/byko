Services · Specification §7

Invoices can be settled in BYKO

The author accepts BYKO in payment for his own work. The price is set in euro; the token is only how that price can be paid. This is the one function on the instrument that points forward instead of recording the past.

ParameterValue
Base currencyEuro. Work is quoted in EUR and never in tokens — there is no rate card denominated in BYKO, and there will not be one.
RateThe market rate on the invoice date, taken from the BYKO/USDC pool on Base — the reserve ratio at one specific block, with that block number written on the invoice. Read from the chain, never fixed in advance.
ScopeConsulting, tender and bid preparation, architecture and system audits, and hands-on engineering — in the fields behind 25 years of enterprise work: national tax platforms, SEPA and SWIFT payments, regulated manufacturing, energy, government projects.
Receiving address0xe8fC8769934f9461F7adF6F440ff3883E28021Eb — already one of the listed founder wallets, so anything paid in lands inside the disclosed holdings, visibly.
What happens to the tokensThey are kept. No commitment to convert them, sell them, or support the price follows from accepting them.
No floorBecause the price is in euro, a lower token price simply means more tokens per invoice. Accepting BYKO puts no floor under its price and is not meant to.

This is one person's decision about his own invoices, not a property of the token. It creates no obligation for anyone else, gives holders no rights, and is not a claim that BYKO is worth anything.
